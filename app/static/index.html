Form Check

Pose-based movement & form analysis

Upload a video of a squat (or any full-body movement) and it fits a skeleton to your body frame by frame, then grades the motion — reps, depth, tempo, and left/right symmetry — with form cues. It runs on 2-D pose estimation, no wearables or markers.

    How
    A YOLOv8-pose model locates 17 body keypoints each frame. Joint angles come from the hip–knee–ankle geometry; reps are counted from the knee-angle cycle, and symmetry from the left-vs-right difference. All 2-D, so a clean side-on view reads best.
